# Buckets

| # | Bucket | Evidence from the repo | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Maps & GIS** | `models/campsite.py`, `models/route.py`, PostGIS tables exist since Phase 0 — zero routes, templates, or MapLibre integration reference them. GPX import/parsing was an "open item" in the original concept doc, never built. This is the core product gap. | Large |
| 2 | **UI/UX refresh for 2026** | Clean Blog is a 2016-era Bootstrap 5 template — functional, mobile-responsive, but visually dated. No dark mode, no in-post image galleries, no richer post layout (embedded route map inside a post body), homepage is still a single static masthead rather than a curated landing experience. | Medium-Large |
| 3 | **Content discovery** | `Post.tags` field exists, nothing filters by it. Postgres full-text search was explicitly planned in the original concept doc — never wired to an endpoint or a search box. No pagination (fine at 2 posts, breaks at 20). | Medium |
| 4 | **SEO & syndication** | No `sitemap.xml`, no `robots.txt`, no RSS feed, no per-post OpenGraph/Twitter-card meta tags, no JSON-LD structured data. All flagged as open items back in `post_and_backend.md`, none built. A public blog with zero SEO surface area is currently invisible to search/social by default. | Small-Medium |
| 5 | **Media pipeline maturity** | R2 deferred twice now (`static/uploads/` is git-committed, working, but not what was originally planned). No image optimization — the hero images are full-size JPEGs, no responsive `srcset`, no WebP. Fine at current traffic, a real cost once there are dozens of posts. | Medium |
| 6 | **SQLAdmin for structured data** | The actual UI for entering campsites/routes as structured data (not Markdown) — currently the single biggest reason `app/admin.py` still exists as a stub. Directly unlocks bucket #1. | Medium |
| 7 | **Ops hardening** | Backup verification (`monitoring_ops.md` Phase 4) still open, correctly gated on SQLAdmin shipping. No staging environment, no disaster-recovery drill ever run. Becomes real risk exactly when #6 ships. | Small-Medium |
| 8 | **Performance & accessibility audit** | Never independently measured — no Lighthouse run, no axe accessibility scan. Mobile-first is claimed (by the template's pedigree) but not verified against your actual deployed pages with real content and images. | Small |
| 9 | **Reader engagement** | Currently one-way: no comments, no RSS-based following, no social share buttons. Worth naming since it's a real product decision (stay a personal log, or invite interaction) rather than an oversight — but currently zero infrastructure exists either way. | Medium |
| 10 | **Content volume vs. infra maturity** | 2 real posts exist. Everything from Sveltia to webhooks to Sentry to Dependabot is production-grade for a blog that currently has two entries. Worth naming plainly: the engineering is ahead of the content, and at some point publishing needs to catch up to what's been built to support it. | — |
