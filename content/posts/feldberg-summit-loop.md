# Feldberg Summit Loop

> **Hiking the Black Forest's most scenic and stunning mountain — a proper challenge for two buddies.**

There are hikes, and then there are days in the mountains that turn into stories. The **Feldberg Summit Loop** is meant to be exactly that: a full day out in the Black Forest, two buddies, plenty of elevation, great views, and hopefully a cold beer waiting at the end. 🍺🥾

## 🏔️ The Route

Since this is a proper loop, we **start and finish at the same spot**, making logistics nice and easy.

📍 **Start & Finish:** [Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/VbNfyS8r6tp5NE8J9) **Camping Hochschwarzwald** - Versteegen

According to our planned GPX route:

|  | Route Details |
| --- | --- |
| 🥾 **Distance** | \~25.2 km |
| ⛰️ **Elevation gain** | \~974 m |
| 📉 **Elevation loss** | \~978 m |
| 🏔️ **Highest point** | \~1,482 m |
| 🌲 **Lowest point** | \~658 m |
| 🔄 **Route type** | Loop |
| 💪 **Challenge** | Demanding — a proper full-day hike |

With roughly **25 km and almost 1,000 metres of climbing**, this isn't going to be a casual Sunday stroll.

## 🌲 Into the Black Forest

The plan is simple: leave the car behind, shoulder the backpacks and head into the heart of the **Southern Black Forest**.

The route takes us through the classic Feldberg landscape — dense forest, open mountain terrain, long climbs and, weather permitting, some spectacular views from the highest parts of the route.

At around **1,482 metres**, we'll reach the high point of our loop. That's about as high as you can get in the Black Forest — and we've definitely earned the view by then.

## 👬 Two Buddies. One Mountain.

No race. No records.

Just two buddies, a pair of hiking boots, around **25 kilometres of Black Forest**, and the question of whose idea it was to add almost **1,000 vertical metres** to the day.

The goal?

**Reach the summit. Enjoy the trail. Take too many photos. Get back down. Find beer.** 🍻

Sounds like a plan.
